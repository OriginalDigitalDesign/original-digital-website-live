Gold Circle

Card reveal animation

Brief

Gold Circle were experimenting with an online card betting game, Card Call. The client requested an intro video and a reveal animation for each card in the deck, designed to cut together cleanly for game continuity, resulting in near-photorealistic renders.

Role

3D animation and render, with Travis Baxter (Chromatik Media) on client and project management.
