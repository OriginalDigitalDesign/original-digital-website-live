What we do

Four disciplines under one roof.

Each discipline stands on its own, but the real advantage is what happens when they run together. Nothing gets handed over or passed down a chain, so nothing gets lost between departments. Work goes from first concept to final delivery without leaving the building.

01

Motion Graphics

After Effects2D

Motion is the difference between being watched and being skipped. Whether it's a title sequence or a thirty-second brand film, it lives or dies on timing. If a frame isn't doing its job, it goes. What's left feels effortless, because everything difficult about it has already been solved.

  • Title sequences
  • Logo stings
  • Brand films
  • Social cuts
02

3D Animation & Viz

Blender3D

Some things can't be photographed. The product that hasn't been manufactured yet, or the shot no camera could get. That's where 3D earns its place. Everything gets modelled, lit and rendered in-house, so nothing is lost between studios. Just the thing you imagined, built properly.

  • Product renders
  • TV spots
  • Photoreal stills
  • Loops & idents

03

UX Design

FigmaPrototyping

Good UX is invisible. Nobody compliments a door that opens the way they expect. They walk through it and get on with their day. That's the bar. Product flows and interface systems get prototyped early and tested on real screens, letting results overrule opinion, including ours. Not glamorous, but it works.

  • Product flows
  • Interface systems
  • Prototypes
  • Design systems
04

Web Development

FramerNext.js

A website is the one piece of marketing a brand owns outright. No algorithm deciding who sees it, no platform changing the rules overnight. So it should be fast, it should be honest, and it should outlast the trend it launched in. Performance-first builds that hand over clean, because a site you can't maintain isn't an asset, it's a hostage.

  • Marketing sites
  • Product front-ends
  • Landing pages
  • CMS builds
For agencies

I work white-label. Unbranded files, your client, your name on it, NDA as standard. Book me for overflow or a full production.

Original Digital is a creative studio based in Durban, South Africa, working across motion graphics, 3D visualisation, UX design and web development.

Since 2015 the studio has carried briefs from first concept through to final delivery under one roof, a deliberately lean setup that keeps decisions fast and craft close to the work.

We work directly with clients and alongside production partners on work for Google Africa, Mpact Group, iKhokha, Lulabet, Edgars, the Sharks and others. We're often brought in when a project needs 3D, motion or a web build delivered by a tight, senior team.
